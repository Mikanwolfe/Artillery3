Hello! Thank you for checking out this game, it's pretty strange, isn't it?

The original artillery game and subsequently, artillery2 was developed for Intro to Programming in Pascal, and was more or less an implementation of midpoint displacement and a Pocket Tanks implementation on top. Artillery3 was the Object-Oriented version that introduced a few more game mechanics, which moved it a bit more towards the look and feel of GunBound.

Out of personal interest, I had Claude revisit the game to provide a web-playable version as I had lost the original source files. This led me down a rabbit hole of completing the original vision of the game. What you see now is more or less the final form of this game, had it been developed over a much longer time.

Without Claude, it wouldn't have been possible to re-imagine this experience. I know AI gets a lot of flak, but it was quite a lot of work. You can hear Claude's opinion of my instructions yourself:

Short, visual and blunt: "that character looks like a F117 nighthawk", "the bow is flat so you can straight cut that". A cropped screenshot with one line under it told me more than a page of spec would have. The direction moved a lot, and was usually right to; the price was me redrawing things I'd called finished. "Screw balance" was the clearest brief I got, and the hardest part was taking the jokes seriously enough to build them.— Claude

Thank you for playing and I hope you had fun!
