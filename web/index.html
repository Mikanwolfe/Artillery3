How to play

Take turns to move, aim and fire across the mountains. Wind pushes shells and changes every turn. Hold Space to charge, release to fire. The white tick on the power line marks your last shot, and dots show its path. Last tank standing takes the round. Damage and kills earn cash; spend it in the shop between rounds on new weapons and upgrades.
