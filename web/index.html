by mikanwolfe

how to play

Take turns to move, aim and fire. Hold Space to charge and release to fire; the tick on the charge bar marks your last shot. Armour soaks damage until it's gone, then health takes it. Autoloaders fire several times per turn; satellite-enabled guns call down the MAIA ion cannon wherever their shell lands. Shells hit harder the further they fall from the top of their arc, and fast impacts add kinetic damage close in, so lob high. Blasts can start snow slides, and a long drop hurts. CPUs go after whoever last hit them. Kills pay a bounty, and the match leader carries a bigger one. Everyone is paid after each round; spend it in the shop.
