How to play

Take turns to move, aim and fire. Hold Space to charge and release to fire; the tick on the charge bar marks your last shot. Armour is a second bar on top of health: while you have any, it absorbs a whole hit, however big; then health takes the damage. You can shoot the MAIA satellite to weaken its strikes. Autoloaders fire several times per turn; satellite-enabled guns call down the MAIA ion cannon wherever their shell lands. Shells hit harder the further they fall from the top of their arc, and fast impacts add kinetic damage close in, so lob high. CPUs go after whoever last hit them. Kills pay a bounty, the match leader carries a bigger one, and everyone is paid after each round. With rebalanced weapons, a bought gun reloads for a few of your turns after it fires (more for rarer guns), so a rack of guns lets you fire a big one every turn. Your starter never reloads. The rack at the bottom shows what is loaded.
