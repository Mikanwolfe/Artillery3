How to play

Take turns: move, aim, hold Space to charge, release to fire. Last one standing wins the round.

← →move (uses fuel)
↑ ↓aim (Shift: fine)
Spacehold to charge, release to fire
S · Qswitch weapon (or tap the rack)
Rrepair kit (uses your shot)
Wjump (30% fuel)
1 2 3 4abilities, once bought
Enterend turn
Clickmark a target: shows the power needed
Draglook around
Wheelzoom in and out
M · N · H · Escmute · music · controls · pause
Touchdrag from her to aim · pinch to zoom · MENU to pause

Hints

  • Flak does ×2 to drones and other hostiles
  • Shoot MAIA down to take her offline for 2 turns
  • Lob high: a long fall adds up to +50%
  • Armour soaks a whole hit while any is left
  • Bought guns reload: carry several and rotate
  • CPUs go after whoever last hit them
